ARISTOCREEKERS — APPROVING PUBLIC SUBMISSIONS

New suggestions are stored privately until you approve them.

1. Sign in to Supabase and open the AristoCreekers project.
2. Open Table Editor from the left side.
3. Open the venues table.
4. Review the venue name, city, state, website, and other details.
5. Change approved from false to true for a venue you want displayed.
6. Open the recommendations table.
7. Review the description and Creeker Tip.
8. Change approved from false to true for recommendations you want displayed.

A venue must be approved before its approved recommendations can appear.
If another person recommends an existing venue, the new recommendation is
added to that venue's profile after you approve the recommendation.

Never share a Supabase secret key, service_role key, or database password.
The publishable key used by the website is intended to be public.
